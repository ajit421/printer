Print From Anywhere in the World

To print from outside your home Wi-Fi (e.g. from your phone at work or college), you can expose this Glassy Hub securely without port forwarding using Cloudflare Tunnels:

Step 1: Install cloudflared on your Raspberry Pi curl -L --output cloudflared.deb https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflared/releases/latest/download/cloudflared-linux-arm64.deb
sudo dpkg -i cloudflared.deb
Step 2: Start an instant secure HTTPS tunnel cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5000

💡 Cloudflare gives you a free, public https://your-printer.trycloudflare.com URL that you can open from any mobile phone or browser worldwide!
