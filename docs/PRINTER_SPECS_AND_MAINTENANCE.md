## 🧼 Official Printhead Cleaning Sequences

*(Transcribed directly from the warning/troubleshooting label D03948001 on the printer)*

### Procedure 1: Normal Cleaning
Use this if faint lines, missing dots, or poor print quality appear on test prints.

1. **Initiate Cleaning Mode**:
   - Press the **Stop** (Power) button and **Copy Shortcut** button simultaneously.
   - The **Power LED**, **Paper Warning LED**, and **Ink LED** will all illuminate for 1 second.
   - Next, the **Ink LED** starts flashing.
2. **Execute Color Selection**:
   - **All Four Colors**: Press the **Colour Copy** button.
   - **Black Only**: Press the **Mono Copy** button.
3. The machine performs cleaning and automatically prints a **Print Quality Check Sheet**.

---

### Procedure 2: Special (Deep / Force) Cleaning
Use this if normal cleaning has been executed several times without resolving missing nozzle lines.

> [!WARNING]
> Special cleaning consumes a large volume of ink. Ensure ink levels are sufficient before starting!

1. **Pre-check**: Ensure all 4 ink tanks are filled **more than halfway**.
2. **Step 1**: Press the **Stop** (Power) button and **Copy Shortcut** button simultaneously.
3. **Step 2**: Immediately press and hold **Mono (Black) Copy** and **Colour Copy** simultaneously for **5 seconds**.
4. The printer will begin heavy motorized pump suction and printhead purging.

## 🧪 Ink Supply System (BTD100 Series)

The DCP-T236 utilizes transparent reservoir chambers accessible via the front-right door:

| Tank | Color | Ink Code | Capacity / Yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BK** | Black | BTD100 BK | High capacity (~7,500 pages yield) |
| **Y** | Yellow | BTD100 Y | Dye-based (~5,000 pages yield combined) |
| **C** | Cyan | BTD100 C | Dye-based (~5,000 pages yield combined) |
| **M** | Magenta | BTD100 M | Dye-based (~5,000 pages yield combined) |

### Refill Guidelines:
1. Open the ink tank cover.
2. Remove the rubber stopper of the selected color.
3. Insert the bottle nozzle securely into the tank opening; do not squeeze forcefully.
4. Replace rubber stopper tightly and close the cover.

## 🎛️ Physical Button Shortcuts

- **Mono Copy**: Single-click to start monochrome photocopy.
- **Colour Copy**: Single-click to start color photocopy.
- **Stop**: Cancels current job or powers down unit.
- **Copy Shortcut**: Preset custom copy profile (e.g. 2-in-1 ID card copy).
- **Scan via Physical Buttons**: Press **Mono Copy** + **Colour Copy** simultaneously to initiate scanner head capture.
